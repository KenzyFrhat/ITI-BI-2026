# 📊 The Commute Question --- Statistics Case Study

> **ITI \| Introduction to Statistics --- Case Study 01**

A practical statistics case study using **real-shaped, deliberately
messy survey data**.

The goal is to go beyond calculations and make **evidence-based
decisions**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Case Study Objective

ITI surveyed \~**260 students across 5 branches** to answer:

-   🚍 Is the commute problem real?
-   📈 Does commuting affect performance?
-   🎯 Who should qualify for a possible remote-attendance policy?

## 📌 Key Statistical Principles

> **Error ≠ Outlier**\
> Remove impossible values. Keep genuine extreme observations.

> **Correlation ≠ Causation**\
> A relationship alone does not prove that commuting causes lower
> performance.

> **Numbers need context**\
> The right statistic depends on the variable, distribution, and
> question.
